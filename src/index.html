04 / Background

Research &
technology transfer

I work between academic research and production engineering: identifying the right questions, developing the theory, and translating the results into systems that work.

I lead distributed systems research at Mysten Labs / Sui. Previously, I was an Assistant Professor at IST Austria and, before that, a Research Scientist at Facebook / Meta. I earned my PhD in Computer Science at EPFL and my MEng at the National Technical University of Athens.

My focus is distributed computing and applied cryptography, with contributions to consensus, execution, storage, transparency, and threshold cryptography.
